# Contributing to Wellspent

Bug reports, feature requests, and discussions are welcome through
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/benschac/timer/issues).

## Code and documentation contributions

Wellspent offers project-owned code under `AGPL-3.0-only` and separately negotiated
[commercial licenses](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md). To preserve both options, discuss
contributions with the maintainer before submitting copyrightable code,
documentation, or assets.

Before merging such a contribution, the maintainer must obtain a separate written
agreement from the relevant rights holder permitting distribution under both the
AGPL and proprietary commercial terms. Employer-owned work requires authorization
from the employer. This repository does not yet provide a contributor agreement
or an automated signing process; the agreement must be arranged before merge.

Submitting a pull request does not itself grant commercial relicensing rights,
transfer copyright, or constitute acceptance of an unwritten agreement.
An AGPL-only contribution must not be merged into code offered under the
commercial licensing option without the necessary additional permission.

Identify any third-party material and preserve its copyright and license notices.
Do not offer rights you do not hold. The maintainer must review whether that
material can be included under the project's licensing model.
